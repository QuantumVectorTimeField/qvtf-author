📖 User Guide & Reference Manual

LaTeX & Markdown Helper & Publisher documentation, features, and keyboard shortcuts.

🚀 Quick Start

Opening Files: Click Open Document in the top header, choose from Open Recent, or simply drag & drop any .tex or .md file from Finder into the window.

Creating Documents: Click New Document (Cmd+N) to start with a clean template.

Theme Switcher: Toggle between Dark Theme, Light Academic, and System Auto in the top bar.

Draggable Splitters: Hover over dividers between panes to drag and resize the Editor, Live Preview, and Sidebar widths.

📝 Document Editor & Navigation

Line Numbers: Enable or disable line numbering via the Show Line Numbers toggle.

Find & Replace: Press Cmd+F / Ctrl+F to open the search bar. Supports forward/backward navigation and single/batch replace.

Right-Click Menu: Right-click inside the editor for Cut, Copy, Paste, Select All, and Scan Spelling shortcuts.

👁️ Live Split-Screen Document Preview

Click Split Preview to toggle real-time rendering beside your editor.

Live Auto-Update: Edits update automatically as you type with smooth scroll-sync and zero screen flickering.

LaTeX Mathematics: Equations, matrices, theorem environments, and inline math ($...$, \[...\], align, gather) are typeset in sharp vector MathML / MathJax.

🔍 Multi-Language Spell Checker

The spellchecker scans your text while intelligently ignoring LaTeX commands, citations, and math environments.

10 Languages: Select from UK English, US English, Canadian English, Australian English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch.

Force -ise: Automatically detects and flags American -ize spelling when UK English is active.

Resolutions: Click any error to jump to its location, pick localized suggestions, replace all occurrences, ignore, or add to your custom dictionary.

⚠️ Compiler Diagnostics & Error Resolver

When compiling LaTeX, compiler messages are parsed and mapped back to the original source line.

Smart Fixes: Detects common issues (missing items in lists, text-mode math, unescaped characters, missing packages) and offers 1-click Apply Suggested Fix.

📤 Publishing & Export Workflows

PDF Export: Supports LuaLaTeX, XeLaTeX, and PDFLaTeX with custom bibliography (.bib), CSL citation styles, margins, and equation numbering (global, chapter, section).

HTML5 Export: Generates standalone, responsive HTML5 pages with native offline vector MathML or Substack/Medium MathJax JS.

LaTeX Source (.tex): Converts Markdown to pure standalone LaTeX ready for journal submission.

1-Click Presets: Quickly apply presets for Formal PDF (Print/arXiv), Substack HTML, Medium HTML, and LaTeX Source.

📜 Preambles Manager

Click Preambles to save, manage, and insert reusable LaTeX package headers, macros, theorems, and styling presets into your documents.
